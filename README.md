# The-Living-Realm-Spreadsheet-Game
An RPG game where you visit villagers, report your daily live such as doing real-life quest that you make and finished, reporting looted and spent gold, and much more!

## What you can do
- Analyze your talent [(Wizzy's service)](https://github.com/aryasatyaardy-cell/The-Living-Realm-Spreadsheet-Game/blob/main/Wizzy%20the%20Talented%20Wizard.md)
- Mark important date on calendar [(Orlo's service)](https://github.com/aryasatyaardy-cell/The-Living-Realm-Spreadsheet-Game/blob/main/Orlo%20the%20Timekeeper.md)
- Record your spending [(Goober's service)](https://github.com/aryasatyaardy-cell/The-Living-Realm-Spreadsheet-Game/blob/main/Goober%20the%20Gold%20Hoarder.md)
- Wishlist items and get it calculated if you can purchase it or if not, how much dept you'd be in [(Frohp's service)](https://github.com/aryasatyaardy-cell/The-Living-Realm-Spreadsheet-Game/blob/main/Frohp%20the%20Shopkeeper.md)
- 12-month daily habit tracker [(Shick's service)](https://github.com/aryasatyaardy-cell/The-Living-Realm-Spreadsheet-Game/blob/main/Shick%20the%20Observant.md)
- To do list, categorized by urgency and completion lvl [(Piqy's service)](https://github.com/aryasatyaardy-cell/The-Living-Realm-Spreadsheet-Game/blob/main/Piqy%20the%20Swifty.md)
- Project tracker to see how much in each percentage progress, finished, or pendingwith your money [(Ract's service)](https://github.com/aryasatyaardy-cell/The-Living-Realm-Spreadsheet-Game/blob/main/Ract%20the%20Blue%20Collar.md)
